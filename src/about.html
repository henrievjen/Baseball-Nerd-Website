About Baseball Nerd

Your free, fast MLB companion app

What Is Baseball Nerd?

Baseball Nerd is a free, ad-supported web application and Android app built for Major League Baseball fans who want fast, clean access to real-time MLB data — without the clutter of a traditional sports portal. Every feature on the site pulls live data directly from the official MLB Stats API, so scores, standings, player statistics, and team schedules are always current and accurate.

The site covers all 30 MLB franchises across both the American League and National League — from the New York Yankees and Los Angeles Dodgers to the Oakland Athletics and Tampa Bay Rays. Whether you follow one team obsessively or track the whole league, Baseball Nerd gives you a clean, modern interface to do it.

Features

  • Live Scores — Real-time MLB scores with inning-by-inning breakdowns, pitching matchups, and final results for every game each day.
  • Standings — Full AL and NL standings updated daily, including wins, losses, winning percentage, games behind, run differential, home/away splits, last-10 records, and wild card standings.
  • Player Stats — Career and season statistics for every active MLB player. Batting: AVG, OBP, SLG, OPS, HR, RBI, SB. Pitching: ERA, WHIP, K, BB, IP, W, L, SV.
  • Team Schedules — Full season schedules for all 30 teams with starting pitcher matchups, past results, and upcoming games.
  • Depth Charts — Organizational depth charts from the big-league roster down through each minor league affiliate — essential for fantasy baseball managers.
  • Stadium Weather — Live weather at all 30 MLB ballparks: temperature, wind speed and direction, humidity, and precipitation chance.
  • Player Search — Search the complete MLB player database by name for full career profiles, headshots, and current team info.

The Creator

Hi! I'm Henri Evjen, the creator and sole developer of Baseball Nerd. I'm a lifelong baseball fan, avid card collector, and software developer based in the United States. I built this site to solve a simple problem: I wanted a fast, distraction-free way to check scores and stats on my phone without wading through pop-ups, auto-play videos, and endless ads.

Baseball Nerd started as a personal project and grew into something I'm genuinely proud of. Every feature you see was designed and coded by me — from the live score cards to the detailed player profiles. The site runs entirely on the official MLB Stats API (no scraping) and is updated regularly with new features based on community feedback.

Outside of Baseball Nerd, I've been collecting baseball cards since I was a kid. I primarily collect vintage cards from the 1950s–1980s as well as modern autograph and relic cards of current players. If you're a fellow collector, check out my eBay store and CollX profile below.
